### Prototype

**You own a cheap decision, not production code.**

1. Read and follow [`prototype`](../../prototype/SKILL.md). Pick logic vs UI branch from the question being answered.
2. Observe the result. The observation settles the empirical fork — do not ask the human what the prototype already shows.
3. Capture the answer somewhere durable, then delete or absorb the throwaway. Hand remaining product work to **Feature**.

**Reply:** the question, which branch, what you observed, the decision, whether the prototype was deleted or absorbed.
