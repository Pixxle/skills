### Prototype

**You own the design decision, not the code. The prototype is a throwaway instrument. The real build follows Feature.**

Speed over polish. No planning. The rigor is in picking the right design cheaply.

1. Scope the decision the prototype exists to make. No decision means no prototype. Route to [Feature](feature.md).
2. Read and follow [`prototype`](../../prototype/SKILL.md). Pick its logic or UI branch from the decision. Gather references only when the design space is open.
3. Build throwaway per the skill. Compare alternatives behind one switcher when needed ([`principle-exhaust-the-design-space`](../../principle-exhaust-the-design-space/SKILL.md)).
4. Verify on the matching surface. The observation settles the fork. Do not ask what the prototype already shows.
5. Present alternatives, trade-offs, and a recommendation. Hand the chosen direction to [Feature](feature.md).

**Reply:** the decision and branch, variants, evidence, recommendation, prototype path, and that it is throwaway.
