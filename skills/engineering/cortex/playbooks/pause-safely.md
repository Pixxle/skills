### Pause safely

**Explicit stops only.** "Keep going" and full-autonomy language are not a pause. Keep a decision trail via [`handoff`](../../handoff/SKILL.md) instead.

1. Read and follow [`handoff`](../../handoff/SKILL.md) in Pause safely mode.
2. Stop at a safe boundary. No new irreversible action just to pause.
3. Make work durable (`wip:` commit if needed) and write the resume note.

**Reply:** loop position, what's on disk vs only in context, commits, first resume action.
