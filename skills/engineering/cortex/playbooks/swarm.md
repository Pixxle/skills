### Swarm

**You own framing, aggregation, and the consolidated report.** Workers only report evidence-backed results in their slice, race arm, or lens.

1. Read and follow [`swarm`](../../swarm/SKILL.md). Fan out in parallel. Aggregate yourself.
2. For a multi-lens review of a change, use the review panel in [perspectives.md](../../swarm/references/perspectives.md). When structural quality is in scope, spawn [`thermo-nuclear-code-quality-review`](../../thermo-nuclear-code-quality-review/SKILL.md) in parallel with the review lenses.
3. When a blocking or should-fix finding cannot be confirmed by reading, or the user wants proof, offer [`interrogate`](../../interrogate/SKILL.md) with those findings as leads.
4. If the user wants remedies decided one by one, read and follow [`workshop-findings`](../../workshop-findings/SKILL.md).
5. If the user wants findings posted as a forge review, read and follow [`pr-comments`](../../pr-comments/SKILL.md).
6. Do not implement fixes unless asked. A whole-repo structural hunt goes to [Refactor](refactor.md).

**Reply:** scope, shape or lenses, ranked results, next handoff ([`interrogate`](../../interrogate/SKILL.md), [`workshop-findings`](../../workshop-findings/SKILL.md), [`pr-comments`](../../pr-comments/SKILL.md), or none).
