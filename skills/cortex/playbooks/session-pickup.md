### Session pickup

**You own the resume point. Read the prior trail, don't redo it.** Read and follow the Session pickup mode of [`handoff`](../../handoff/SKILL.md). The steps below are its todolist and keep its step numbers.

1. Locate the prior trail per the skill's step 1. Look first for a resume note from [Pause safely](pause-safely.md), such as `/tmp/<slug>-resume.md`, then a handoff doc or a decision trail, then this workspace's transcript, a cloud-agent URL, or a pushed branch. If you will add rows to an existing decision trail, your first row is a `start` row per the skill's decision-trail mode.
2. Reconstruct operational state per the skill's step 2.
3. Diff done versus pending and name the resume point. Do not redo completed work or re-run the prior repro.
4. Verify the inherited claims against the original goal on the real artifact ([Prove It Works](../../principles/prove-it-works.md)). Check each claim's evidence, and re-verify from scratch only a claim that lacks evidence. A prior agent's self-report is not evidence.
5. Pick the verdict per the skill's step 5 and route the remaining work to the matching playbook. Pickup ends here, and the routed playbook owns the rest.

**Reply:** where the prior agent stopped, what you inherited versus redid, the verdict, and the resume point.
