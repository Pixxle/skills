# Exhaust the Design Space

When a UI interaction or an architectural decision has no precedent in the codebase, build 2-3 competing prototypes and compare them side by side before committing.

It also applies to product design decisions where user experience depends on feel, not logic.

For UI, read and follow [`prototype`](../prototype/SKILL.md), which builds the variants behind one switcher, each one labeled. For types, signatures, and module boundaries, read and follow [`architect`](../architect/SKILL.md), which requires at least two structurally distinct sketches before synthesis.
